# Available original research tables

These are unchanged copies of the available school-project workbooks, authorized by Emre for this private archive.

| Workbook | Contents and interpretation |
|---|---|
| [matched_academic_records.xlsx](matched_academic_records.xlsx) | 27,099 data rows, seven columns: Matched Author Name, University, Faculty, Department, Title/Name, Count, Gender. These are linked author/institution records, not 27,099 unique publications. The Gender column contains historical inferred labels, not verified self-reported demographic data. |
| [university_department_links.xlsx](university_department_links.xlsx) | City/university/department/source-link reference table. |
| [university_names_and_departments.xlsx](university_names_and_departments.xlsx) | 208 rows of university-name and department reference information. |

Original names and inferred labels exist in the matched research table; the archive remains private. The presence of these columns does not establish matching accuracy or demographic ground truth. The schema and source hashes are documented in [the full manifest](../../docs/FULL_ARCHIVE_MANIFEST.json).

The full original Scopus publication export (historical scripts mention 1854–2024 ranges), intermediate CSVs and later 2015/2016-separated publication workbooks are not present in the supplied folder. Consequently, this archive does **not** claim to contain the complete raw Scopus corpus or to reproduce every plot end to end. The separate [synthetic example](../synthetic_institution_counts.csv) remains a fabricated educational input; it is not a replacement for the research tables.
